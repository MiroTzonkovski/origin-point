Why more success hasn't fixed it.

Every result in your life sits at the end of a chain. Conditioning sets your state. Your state drives your behavior. Your behavior produces your results.

Most entrepreneurs try to feel better from the bottom of that chain. More revenue. Better habits. A new coach, a new routine, a bigger goal. Then they wait for the result to change how they feel. It can't. The chain only runs one way.

  1. C

    Conditioning Where it starts

    What got wired in before you could choose: the beliefs, the emotions, what you learned you have to do to be worth something.

  2. S

    State

    How you feel day to day. Set by that wiring, not by your bank balance.

  3. B

    Behavior

    How you lead, decide and show up, from that state.

  4. R

    Results Where you've been looking

    The revenue, the wins, the milestones. The end of the chain, not the start of it.

Fulfillment doesn't come from the bottom of the chain. That's why the next milestone never delivers it.
